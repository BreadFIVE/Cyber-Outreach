Welcome, analyst.

A capture-the-flag challenge day on Vulnerability Discovery & Exploitation concepts: CIA, integer bugs, the stack, unsafe C, format strings, the heap, fuzzing and memory protections.

  • Each challenge hides a flag in the format CTF{something_like_this}. Find it, submit it, bank the points.
  • Hints are available but cost points (10% for the first, 30% for the second).
  • You have one clock for everything. Skip hard ones and come back – harder challenges are worth more.
  • The Toolkit tab has a wrap-around calculator and a hex/ASCII converter. A pen and paper help too.
  • Work in teams of 2–4. Explain your reasoning to each other – that's how you learn it.
⚖️ Rules of engagement: This game is about understanding vulnerabilities so we can find and fix them. Everything here is conceptual or simulated. In the real world, only test systems you own or have written permission to test. Unauthorised access is a crime (in the UK, the Computer Misuse Act 1990).

Progress saves in this browser, so refreshing won't lose your score. Use one device per team.

📋 Briefing

Suggested pacing for a 90-minute session:

0–10 minWarm-up. View source, CIA sorting, registers and vocab. 10–40 minSplit up: one on integers, one on the stack and unsafe C, one on fuzzing. 40–75 minFormat strings, the heap and memory protections, then the 200+ point challenges. 75–90 minTake on the Mock Exam Gauntlet and spend hints on anything left.

Flag tips

  • Flags look like CTF{...}. Not case-sensitive, but spelling and underscores matter.
  • Numbers go in plainly, e.g. CTF{44}. Terms use underscores, e.g. CTF{memory_leak}.
  • Interactive challenges reveal their flag once you get every part right.
